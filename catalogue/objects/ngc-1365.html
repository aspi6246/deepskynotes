Background

NGC 1365 is the “Great Barred Spiral Galaxy”, a textbook SB(s)b with a Seyfert 1 nucleus, and one of the showpiece galaxies of the southern sky. At magnitude 9.6 the straight bar shows readily, and dark skies bring out the two arms sweeping back from its ends. It belongs to the Fornax Cluster: the cluster's central elliptical NGC 1399 lies ≈72' away, and NGC 1316 ≈2.4°{} to the south-west. Its recession velocity of 1,522 km/s gives ≈71 million light-years by Hubble's law.
