Background

IC 4628, the “Prawn Nebula,” is a vast emission nebula  6,000 light-years away. Spanning roughly 90' on the sky — three full-Moon diameters — it is one of the largest H ii regions in Scorpius. Discovered by E. E. Barnard around 1900.
