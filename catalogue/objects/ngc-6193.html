Background

NGC 6193 is the young heart of the Ara OB1 association,  4,000 light-years away — a loose cluster whose central pair of hot O stars lights and carves the Rim Nebula (NGC 6188, next page) along the edge of the neighbouring dark cloud. Cluster and nebula share one low-power field, with the tight bright central pair as the visual anchor.

My Observing Notes

44-cm (Club 17.5-inch f/5): In the finder a small bright haze adorned with two bright stars. In the eyepiece a loose collection of 10–20 stars; the bright star at the centre is actually a tight pair, with a much fainter third member floating just above. The cluster sits beside a larger complex of nebulosity stretching across the whole field — NGC 6188, the Rim Nebula, which the cluster itself illuminates. One to revisit with higher magnification and a UHC filter.(Thursday, July 2026)
