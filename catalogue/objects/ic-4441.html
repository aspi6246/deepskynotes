Background

IC 4441 is a small galaxy in Lupus, discovered by Lewis Swift in 1897. Historical confusion surrounds this object: no galaxy was found at Swift's original position, and IC 4441 has since been identified with the nearby IC 4444 — a barred spiral ≈60 million light-years away.
