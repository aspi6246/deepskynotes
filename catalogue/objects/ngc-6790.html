Background

NGC 6790 is a young, compact planetary nebula in Aquila — only about 6,000 years old and roughly a tenth of a light-year across. Its high surface brightness makes it easy to spot despite its tiny apparent size. HST imaging reveals elongated shells surrounding the central white dwarf, which has a temperature of ≈73,500 K.
