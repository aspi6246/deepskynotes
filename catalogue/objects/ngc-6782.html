Background

NGC 6782 is a textbook resonance-ring barred spiral ≈185 million light-years away — its perfect inner ring and theatrical bar made it a poster child for the GALEX ultraviolet mission. At the eyepiece it is a bright-cored 11.8-magnitude oval; the rings are strictly for the photographs, but few galaxies reward looking up an image afterwards so well.
