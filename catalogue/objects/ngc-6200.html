Background

NGC 6200 is a moderately bright open cluster  3,000 light-years away in Ara. Around 70 million years old,  50 members spread across  10' in a relatively open formation.

My Observing Notes

44-cm (Club 17.5-inch f/5): Squeezed into the same field as NGC 6204 with the 31 mm Nagler. The larger ( 10') and sparser of the contrasting pair, sitting on the right of the field.(Saturday, September 2025)

44-cm (Club 17.5-inch f/5): With NGC 6193 centred in the finder, NGC 6200 and 6204 fit together in the 35mm field as a great contrasting pair — 6200 the tight, densely packed one: 30+ stars, with a chain of four brighter stars tapering off the top of the cluster.(Thursday, July 2026)
