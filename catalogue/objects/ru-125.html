Background

Ru 125 and Ru 124 are a pair of faint, sparse open clusters ≈25' apart in southern Scorpius, close enough to share one low-power field. The pairing is optical only: the MWSC survey puts Ru 125 at ≈3,900 light-years and Ru 124 at ≈7,100, with ages differing by a factor of nearly four.

Ru 125 is the larger and looser of the two (≈19', Trumpler III2p); Ru 124 is the smaller and slightly better defined (≈14', II3p). Neither carries an integrated magnitude in Stellarium or SIMBAD, and neither has a Wikipedia entry — catalogue clusters, not showpieces.
