Background

NGC 5824 is a luminous outer-halo globular cluster, one of the most remote in the Galaxy at ≈101,000 light-years from the Sun. About 13 billion years old, it shows no tidal cutoff — cluster stars have been detected out to a radius exceeding 400 parsecs. The Triangulum stellar stream is thought to be tidal debris stripped from NGC 5824.
