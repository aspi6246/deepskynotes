Background

NGC 6907 is the brightest galaxy in Capricornus: a barred spiral ≈150 million light-years away, magnitude 11.3 and 3.5' across. Its northeastern arm hides an interloper — the small lenticular NGC 6908, long mistaken for a piece of the arm and only recognised in 2004 as a distinct companion galaxy currently being devoured.
