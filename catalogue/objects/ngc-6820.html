Background

NGC 6820 is the faint 40' emission field surrounding the young cluster NGC 6823,  6,000 light-years away in the Vulpecula OB1 association. Photographs show a magnificent elephant-trunk pillar pointing at the cluster; visually the nebula is a subtle brightening at low power with an OIII or UHC filter — dark skies essential.
