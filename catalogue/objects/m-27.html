Background

M 27 is the Dumbbell — the first planetary nebula ever discovered (Messier, 1764) and the most easily seen member of the class in the entire sky. ≈1,300 light-years away and a quarter light-year across, its apple-core shape shows in any telescope; an OIII filter fills in the fainter side-lobes that turn the core into a complete football. The 13.5-magnitude central white dwarf is an easy catch in moderate aperture.
