Background

NGC 6204 is a small, dense open cluster in Ara.  6,500 light-years away. About a dozen brighter stars in a tight grouping with a triplet of bright stars hanging off one edge.

My Observing Notes

44-cm (Club 17.5-inch f/5): Sits on the left of a striking contrasting pair with NGC 6200 in the 31 mm Nagler. NGC 6204 is small ( 5') and dense — a dozen brighter stars with a triplet of bright stars hanging off the bottom edge. Lovely combined field.(Saturday, September 2025)

44-cm (Club 17.5-inch f/5): The other half of a contrasting pair with NGC 6200 in the same 35mm field — large, loose and sparse where its neighbour is tight and dense.(Thursday, July 2026)
