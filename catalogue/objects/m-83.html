Background

M83 is the “Southern Pinwheel” (NGC 5236) — a stunning face-on barred spiral  15 million light-years away in Hydra. Its prominent two-arm structure traces vigorous star formation; the galaxy has hosted six historically-observed supernovae, more than almost any other galaxy beyond the Local Group — one of the more violent nearby star factories. At mag 7.5 and nearly 13' across, it presents as a large, soft glow with a bright central bar visible from modest apertures.
