Background

NGC 6026 is a planetary nebula in Lupus with a close binary central star — one of relatively few confirmed cases. The pair orbits with a period of just 0.53 days, and one component nearly fills its Roche lobe, producing an ellipsoidal light variation visible in time-resolved photometry.
