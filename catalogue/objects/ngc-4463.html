Background

NGC 4463 is a small, faint open cluster in Musca, ≈3,500 light-years away. Only ≈3' across with a tight knot of brighter stars at its core.

My Observing Notes

25-cm (Meade 10-inch LX200): Tough to pick out at 9th magnitude in the finderscope. In the eyepiece appears as a small (3') cluster, with a dense knot of 7–10 brighter stars in a tight formation almost resembling an arrow pointing left.(Saturday, August 2025)
