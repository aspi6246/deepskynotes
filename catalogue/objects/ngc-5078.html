Background

NGC 5078 is a near-edge-on early-type spiral  80 million light-years away in Hydra, prominent for the thin dust lane cutting across its bright elongated bulge. A small companion (IC 879) sits  4' to the south, just visible in larger apertures. NGC 5078 forms an attractive pair with the rounder, face-on NGC 5101 0.5° to the east-southeast — both fit in a single low-power field.
