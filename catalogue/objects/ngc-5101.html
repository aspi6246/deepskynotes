Background

NGC 5101 is a face-on barred lenticular galaxy ≈90 million light-years away in Hydra, classified SB(rs)0/a. At mag 10.5 it presents as a low-surface-brightness disk with a bright nucleus and a partial inner ring. Often paired with the more dramatic edge-on NGC 5078 just 0.5° to the west-northwest — together they make a fine double-galaxy field in the same low-power eyepiece.
