Background

IC 5068 is the forgotten third sheet of the North America/Pelican complex, hanging south of the Pelican across yet another dark lane. It has no shape anyone bothered to name — just a very low-surface-brightness field of emission that OIII and a wide field render as a subtle brightening. Strictly for dark-sky nights, and satisfying precisely because nobody looks at it.
