Background

UGC 11657 is the brighter component of the interacting pair KPG 551,  275 million light-years away in Aquarius, with UGC 11658 just under 1' to its north-east. Its recession velocity of 5,901 km/s sits within about 70 km/s of its neighbour's — that shared redshift is what marks the two as a genuinely bound system rather than a chance line-of-sight alignment. An emission-line galaxy at magnitude 13.5 and barely 37” across, it is the brighter of the pair only by comparison.
