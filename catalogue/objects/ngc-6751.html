Background

NGC 6751 is the “Glowing Eye Nebula” — a planetary nebula  6,500 light-years away in Aquila, discovered by Albert Marth in 1863. About 0.8 light-years in diameter, its concentric shells of expelled gas are ionised by a central star at  140,000 K. HST imaging reveals a striking ring structure that gives the nebula its nickname.
