Background

IC 1392 is an elliptical/lenticular galaxy ≈200 million light-years away, glimpsed through the Cygnus star-veil as a 13th-magnitude round glow. One of the more improbable objects in this chapter — a galaxy hunted through one of the richest star fields in the sky.
