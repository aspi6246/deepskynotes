Background

M 73 is four unrelated stars — Messier logged it in 1780 as a “cluster of four stars with nebulosity”, and astronomers spent two centuries deciding whether it was a real system; proper-motion studies finally settled it as a chance asterism in the early 2000s. The Y-shaped quartet sits 1.5° east of M 72, worth two minutes for the story alone.
