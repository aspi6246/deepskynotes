Background

M 29 is a compact quadrilateral of 8th-magnitude stars — a “cooling tower” or miniature dipper south of γ Cygni. It sits behind some of the heaviest dust in the Cygnus rift, which dims it by around three magnitudes: without that veil it would rank with the showpiece Messier clusters instead of being the catalogue's modest oddity.
