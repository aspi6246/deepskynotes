Background

NGC 7582 is a barred spiral galaxy with a Seyfert 2 nucleus whose activity is hidden behind dust. It is the brightest member of the Grus Quartet, with NGC 7590 ≈10' and NGC 7599 ≈13' away and NGC 7552 ≈28' to the south-west. Its recession velocity of 1,570 km/s places it ≈73 million light-years away. At magnitude 10.6 the elongated ≈4' disk and brighter bar show well, and a medium-power eyepiece takes in the three nearest galaxies together.
